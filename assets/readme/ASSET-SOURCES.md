# Asset sources

## Terminal 3 logo

- Source: https://terminal3.io/ (homepage navigation logo, retrieved 18 November 2026).
- Official brand guidelines: https://live.standards.site/terminal3
- `terminal3-logo.svg` contains the official icon and outlined wordmark paths extracted from the website, with its CSS colors resolved to their published values: `#131e33`, `#8dc6ea`, and `#475467`.
- The same artwork appears on a white panel in the overview banner. Logo geometry and proportions are preserved.
- Terminal 3 trademarks remain the property of their owner.
